Von Bertalanffy Mathematical Engine

Aquatic bioenergetics integrated with real-time environmental scalars:

W(t) = W_inf * [ 1 - exp(-K * E * (t - t_0)) ] ^ b
W_inf (Asymptotic Max Weight)4,500.0 g
K (Intrinsic Curvature Param)0.380 /yr
t_0 (Theoretical Age at L=0)-0.150 yr
b (Length-Weight Exponent)3.020 (Isometric)

Calibration Status

SCREENING ESTIMATE (Tier 1): Parameters from FAO / FishBase generic subtropical riverine benchmarks. Hydrochemical profiles derived from seasonal baseline models of the lower Rupsha-Pasur system.

Environmental Coupling Logic

Growth velocity is scaled multiplicatively by temperature (fT), dissolved oxygen availability (fDO), osmotic energy loss from salinity (fS), and acid-base metabolic stress (fpH).
