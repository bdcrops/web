BDCrops Technologies is a Bangladesh-based AgriTech company operating two flagship brands: 🌾 BDCrops (satellite intelligence for agriculture) and 🤖 PlayOwnAI (AI innovation platform with 9 specialized business units).

Our Story

Empowering organizations with data-driven agricultural intelligence

Our mission is to empower governments, agribusinesses, financial institutions, NGOs, researchers, and farmers with accurate, data-driven insights that improve agricultural productivity while reducing climate-related risks.

By combining satellite imagery, Artificial Intelligence, machine learning, GIS, weather analytics, and crop simulation models, BDCrops delivers actionable intelligence that helps organizations make faster and smarter decisions.
