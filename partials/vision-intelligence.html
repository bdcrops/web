Per-Unit Assembly of Shared Parts — cf2.core.vision

src/cf2/core/vision/
├── contracts/ FrameContext, Detection, BoundingBox, TrackedObject, VisionAlert
├── ingestion/ file_source (GazeData), rtsp_source (Surveillance/Traffic/Drone), satellite_source (Highway Billboard)
├── detectors/ registry get_detector("person", version="best") — Haar/HOG → YOLOv8/RT-DETR without touching Units
├── tracking/ tracker.py dwell, loitering, unique count, speed
├── biometrics.py silhouette demographics estimated labeled, facial embeddings
├── behavior.py optical flow, aggression, crowd density
├── thermal.py IR/thermal hotspot
├── geospatial/ camera_calibration, drone_telemetry GPS/gimbal → lat-lon, satellite_calibration GSD/tile
├── runtime/ daemon.py long-lived per-feed, edge_sync.py offline queue, health.py reconnect/backpressure
├── governance/ privacy_tiers PUBLIC/PRIVATE/GOV_RESTRICTED, redaction auto face-blur, audit_log chain-of-custody
└── events/ dispatcher webhook, MQTT, dashboard, SMS/radio
