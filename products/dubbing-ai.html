Frequently Asked Questions

How does DubbingAI differ from basic AI text-to-speech generators?

Basic TTS tools only generate plain audio files. DubbingAI is a complete video translation and visual reconstruction engine that analyzes on-screen code/slides, synchronizes speech timing to exact video frame boundaries, and can rebuild outdated video footage into modern 3D digital lessons.

Can DubbingAI accurately handle programming code and technical jargon?

Yes. DubbingAI uses visual OCR intelligence to extract on-screen code, terminal commands, and technical slide notes. This ensures that the generated narration correctly pronounces function names, variables, and technical concepts across different languages.

What video formats and aspect ratios are supported?

DubbingAI supports standard 16:9 HD/4K landscape outputs for YouTube and LMS platforms, as well as 9:16 vertical video reflow for Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.

Is my video content and brand voice secure?

Absoutely. BDCrops operates enterprise-grade encryption and strict privacy protocols. Your video assets and cloned voice models remain 100% private to your workspace and are never shared or used to train public models.
