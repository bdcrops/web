What we deliver

✅ e-GP + NGO daily scraping

We scrape e-GP, UNDP, World Bank tenders daily. Problem: Checking e-GP daily wastes 2 hours. Solution: Daily email with relevant tenders.

✅ BOQ & spec auto-extraction

We extract items, quantities from PDF. Problem: 100-page PDF takes day to read. Solution: Excel sheet with all items in 2 minutes.

We check if you qualify for tender. Problem: Apply then rejected for missing trade license. Solution: Instant eligibility check.
